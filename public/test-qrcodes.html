📋 How to Use in Presentation

  1. Open RTSA Dashboard (rtsa-dashboard.html) and connect MetaMask
  2. Open Police Verification (verify.html) in another tab/window
  3. In Police UI, enter a test QR code (e.g., Zq123, EXP001, or FAKE123)
  4. Click "Verify License" - request sent to RTSA dashboard in real-time
  5. In RTSA dashboard, click "Process Request" to verify
  6. Result appears instantly in Police UI!

🎯 Pro Tip for Presentation: Use the Quick Test buttons on the Police UI for fastest demonstration!
